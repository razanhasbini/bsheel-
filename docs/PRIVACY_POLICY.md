# Privacy Policy — Quest App

**Last updated:** 2026-03-25

## What data we collect
- **Account data:** Email, username, display name, profile picture (optional)
- **Quest data:** Quest completions, submissions (photos/videos), reaction history
- **Device data:** Device type and OS version for analytics

## How we use your data
- To operate the app and provide quest features
- To display your profile, submissions, and leaderboard rank to other users
- To send you in-app notifications about quest status

## Data storage
- All data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS) with row-level security
- Media files (avatars, submission photos/videos) are stored in Supabase Storage
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext

## Data sharing
- We do not sell your data to third parties
- Your public profile (username, display name, avatar, XP, level) is visible to other app users
- Approved quest submissions are visible in the public feed

## Your rights
- You can delete your account and all associated data at any time
- You can update or remove your profile picture
- You can contact us to request a data export

## Contact
For privacy questions, contact the app developer.
