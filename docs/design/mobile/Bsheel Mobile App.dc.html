NEW QUESTS DROPPED
THE APPEAL FLOW HANGS ON ONE NOTIFICATION
Three things route to the submission status page: a notification tap, the Quest-of-the-Day ticket stub after a first-time rejection, and the redirect straight after submitting. Two of those are transient. If push delivery breaks, a rejected user has no way to find the appeal. The QUEST HISTORY frame above adds a fourth, durable route — a REJECTED row with its own APPEAL button — which is the single highest-value change in this set.
READ AND UNREAD
Unread keeps its 2px ink border and hard shadow. Read drops the shadow and thins the border, but the fill stays white — dimming the text would punish someone for having already looked. Colour is reserved for what the notification is about, never for whether it has been opened.

BUTTONS · 44PT MINIMUM
Shadow depth signals weight: 5px primary, 4px secondary, none when disabled. Disabled loses its shadow and takes a dashed border, so it reads as unavailable rather than just dim.

Category tags are square-ish at 8px; status pills are fully round. The shape difference means you can tell them apart before you read them.

METERS · TIMERS · AVATARS
02:47:11 00:04:52
Timers are always mono and tabular so digits don't jitter. Under five minutes the countdown takes a coral chip. Avatars are circular for people, rounded-square for your own profile tile.
BOTTOM NAV · 5 TABS
Active tab is ink, inactive is soft ink — no fill, no pill. Collab is reached from Feed rather than the nav; five tabs is already the ceiling.
MEDIA PLACEHOLDER · SKELETON
PROOF MEDIA
Skeletons keep the 2px border but drop to a muted violet-grey, so a loading card still reads as a card and nothing shifts when the data lands.
