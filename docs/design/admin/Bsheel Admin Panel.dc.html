BSHEEL PRIVACY POLICY
PRIVACY POLICY
LAST UPDATED 04 MAR 2025
Bsheel stores the account details you give us, the quests you complete, and the photo or video proof you submit. Proof media is kept in private storage and served only through short-lived signed links.
WHAT MODERATORS SEE
A moderator reviewing your submission sees your username, level, the proof and caption you submitted, and your prior approval history. They do not see your email address or device identifiers.
DELETION
You can delete your account from Settings or from the public deletion page. Removal completes within 48 hours and cannot be reversed.

SCENARIOS
Every non-happy path a moderator or player can land in
LOADING · SKELETON
Shapes match the real rows so nothing jumps on load. No spinner — the queue is never blank for long enough to earn one.
EMPTY · QUEUE CLEARED
✓
ALL CLEAR
No pending submissions.
Every empty state names one thing worth doing next rather than ending the road.
ERROR · RETRY
FAILED TO LOAD
The queue didn't come back. Nothing was lost — no decision has been recorded.
Errors say what did not happen. A moderator's first fear is a half-applied decision.
OFFLINE
OFFLINE
Reconnecting… decisions are queued locally.
A persistent bar, not a toast. Approve and Reject stay tappable and sync when the connection returns.
ACCOUNT SUSPENDED / BANNED
ACCOUNT SUSPENDED
New quests are paused. Reach out to support.
The QOTD ticket is hidden entirely, not disabled — a greyed CTA invites a tap that the server would reject anyway.
BLOCKED USER
tarek_s
You blocked this account. Their posts and comments are hidden from your feed, and yours from theirs.
Blocks cut both directions. The profile is reachable but stripped, so a block never reads as a deleted account.
RATE LIMIT · REROLLS SPENT
NO REROLLS LEFT
You've used all 5 for today. The next one unlocks in 6h 14m — pick from what you rolled, or wait.
The limit is server-authoritative, so the client states the real reset time rather than hiding the button.
DELETED / HIDDEN POST
POST REMOVED FROM FEED
Still visible on your profile. Add it back any time.
Hidden and deleted read differently: hidden is reversible and says so, permanent deletion never offers an action.
FIRST RUN · ZERO DATA
ROLL YOUR FIRST QUEST
No stats yet, no streak, no history. One button, nothing else competing with it.
Stat tiles, XP meter and streak card are suppressed at zero rather than shown reading 0 — an empty scoreboard is discouraging on day one.
